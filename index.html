What I build

Salesforce is a strong database for an organization, but its screens are not always right for the public or for busy staff. I build the pages around it.

1

Public forms and payments

Registration, donation and signup pages that create Contacts, Campaign Members and Opportunities in Salesforce, take payment through your gateway and let people resume where they left off.

2

Staff portals and case tools

Focused screens for case workers, coordinators and volunteers: search, review, update, attach documents and see only what each role needs. Sign-in through your own Google accounts.

3

Contracts and signatures

Applications, interviews and agreements filled in on screen, signed on a phone or tablet and filed back to the right Salesforce record.

4

Integrations and automation

Connect donation platforms, email tools, receipting and exchange-rate feeds so data moves on its own. Scheduled reports land in the right inbox.

5

Reports your team can open

Year-to-date giving, campaign summaries and coordinator reports in a clean page, without building a dashboard for every question.

6

Beyond Salesforce

Where Salesforce is not the right fit, I build on a standard database such as MySQL, or sync the two.
